What Eyva is

Eyva is a Pomodoro study timer that uses your webcam to look for visual signs of drowsiness, such as closed eyes, yawning, and a dropped head, and reminds you to take a break. Its alerts and scores are visual indications only. They are not a medical assessment and do not measure focus or productivity.

Not medical advice

Do not rely on Eyva for health, safety, or medical decisions, and do not use it while driving or operating machinery. If you often feel very sleepy, talk to a health professional.
